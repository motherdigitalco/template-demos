Our Story

Baked like it
matters, because it does.

Butter & Bloom started in 2016 with a home oven, a stand mixer, and a stubborn belief: a celebration cake should taste as good as it looks. Everything is baked from scratch with real butter, farm eggs, and seasonal fruit. No mixes, no frozen layers, no shortcuts.

  • Baked fresh every morning, never frozen
  • Real butter, real vanilla, seasonal fruit
  • Custom designs sketched with you, not from a catalog

- Marigold Lane, founder & head baker

Lead Times

Standard custom cakes need 7 days notice. Two-tier and sculpted cakes need 14 days. Wedding cakes book 3 to 6 months ahead, especially May through October. Rush orders are sometimes possible for a 25% fee, just ask.

Deposit & Payment

A 50% non-refundable deposit reserves your date. The balance is due at pickup or delivery. We accept cards, cash, and bank transfer. Your date is not held until the deposit is paid.

Allergy Notice

Allergy disclaimer: our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts, and soy. While we take care with every order, we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment. Please tell us about every allergy in the inquiry form so we can advise you honestly.
