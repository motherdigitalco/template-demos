Safety First

If there's an emergency.

Emergencies are rare, but we plan like they aren't. Here is exactly what happens if your pet needs urgent care on our watch.

  1. Secure your pet and assess. We calm the situation first, then check breathing, bleeding, and responsiveness.
  2. Call your veterinarian. Your vet's number from the intake form is our first call.
  3. Contact you immediately. We call and text every number on file until we reach you.
  4. Transport to the emergency vet clinic on file if care is urgent. Your pet is never left waiting when minutes matter.
  5. We stay with your pet and document everything. You get a full written account of what happened and what was done.

Your permission matters

The emergency consent checkbox in the Pet Intake form authorizes us to seek urgent veterinary care if you cannot be reached. Without it, we will still secure your pet and keep trying to contact you.

Keys & Access

Your keys, handled carefully.

Getting into your home should be the easiest part of the whole arrangement. Pick whichever option suits you best, and we take it from there.

  • Lockbox code (our favorite): we bring our own lockbox or use yours.
  • Key pickup at the meet & greet: we collect a copy before the first visit.
  • Doorman or building access: add us to your approved visitor list and we're in.
  • Keys are tagged with your pet's name only, never your address.
  • Keys are returned at the final visit, or kept on file with your permission for easy rebooking.
  • We never hide keys under mats, in planters, or in fake rocks.
