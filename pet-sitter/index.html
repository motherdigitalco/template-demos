About Us

The family behind the shine

Spark & Shine started in 2019 with one vacuum, a borrowed minivan, and a belief that a clean home should not be a luxury. Seven years later we are still family-run, still local, and still the crew that texts you when we are on the way.

Every cleaner is background-checked, trained in our own homes first, and paid a real living wage. Happy cleaners do careful work. That is the whole business model.

Good to Know

Supplies & policies

The fine print, minus the fine print. Short, plain, and fair.

Supplies policy

We bring everything: HEPA vacuums, microfiber cloths, and eco-friendly products that are safe around kids and pets. You do not need to provide a thing.

Prefer we use your products? Just say so in the estimate form. Have a favorite vacuum or a specialty stone cleaner? We will happily use yours and skip ours.

Cancellation & access

Reschedule free with at least 48 hours notice. Inside 48 hours, a $45 visit fee applies because your crew already turned down other work.

Access: leave a key, a door code, or be home to let us in. If we cannot get in, the visit counts as a late cancellation. Pets are fine. Just tell us who is friendly and who needs a closed door.
