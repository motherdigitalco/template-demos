How it works

From first call to final print.

A straight line, no runaround. You'll always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We talk — your lot, your budget, your must-haves. Free 30-minute call. If I'm not the right man for the job, I'll tell you straight.

  2. 02

    Draft

    I draw the first full set: floor plans, elevations, foundation, and roof. You review real drawings, not vague promises.

  3. 03

    Revise

    We move walls, resize rooms, and sweat the details until it's right. Revisions are part of the price, not a surprise invoice.

  4. 04

    Build

    You get the final permit-ready set — PDF and CAD. Hand it to your builder or your building department with confidence.
