Our Story

From green bean
to your cup.

Copper Cup started in 2018 with a secondhand roaster and a stubborn belief: coffee tastes better when the distance from roast to cup is measured in days, not months. We buy from fourteen smallholder farms, roast in 12-kilo batches, and never let a bean sit longer than three weeks.

  • Roasted on-site every Tuesday & Friday
  • Direct-trade lots from 14 origins
  • Pastries baked in-house each morning

— June & Marcus Hale, founders
