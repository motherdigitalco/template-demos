About the Cook

Hi, I'm June.

I cook the way my grandmother did — by feel, with what's in season, and always enough to share. This little corner of the internet is where I write it all down: the weeknight dinners that got us through soccer season, the breads I bake when the house feels too quiet, and the preserves I put up every August without fail.

I'm not a chef. I'm a mom with a well-loved Dutch oven, a garden that mostly cooperates, and strong opinions about butter. Everything here is tested in my real kitchen, with real timing, real messes, and honest notes about what I'd do differently.

  • Every recipe tested at least 3 times
  • Realistic timing — no "30-minute" meals that take an hour
  • Substitutions noted for picky eaters & allergies

— June
